My story

Why Veridian exists.

I trained across the full digital marketing stack: building websites from scratch, search engine optimisation, web analytics, email marketing, Google Search and Display advertising, social media, and Facebook and Instagram ads. Every skill was learned by doing it on a live website, not in theory.

I started Veridian Growth for a simple reason: good businesses lose customers every day to competitors who are simply easier to find online. Not better, just more visible. That's fixable, and fixing it is the work I enjoy most.

So I built Veridian Growth to be one specialist covering everything. Instead of juggling a web designer, an SEO agency and an ads freelancer, you work with one person who sees the whole picture and is accountable for all of it.

I'm based in Lagos, Nigeria, and work remotely across time zones, planning calls around your working day. You'll always deal with me directly.
