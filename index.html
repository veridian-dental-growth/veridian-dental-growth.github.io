The reality

Patients choose the practice they find first.

Great dentistry isn't enough if patients can't find you, can't book easily, or never hear from you again after their last visit.

01

Search decides

When someone needs a check-up, an emergency appointment or a smile they're proud of, they search Google and shortlist the practices at the top. If you're not there, you're not considered.

02

Empty chairs are expensive

Every gap in the diary is time your team is paid for and your practice doesn't earn from. Lapsed patients who never rebook quietly add to it.

03

The rules are strict

Dental advertising is held to GDC guidance and the CAP Code. Generic marketers often miss them, which can lead to ASA complaints and professional risk.

Compliance-first

Marketing that respects the rules you're held to.

Every piece of work is checked against the advertising standards UK dental professionals must follow before it goes live.

This is a marketing review, not legal advice. For complex questions, I'll always suggest you check with your dental defence organisation.

  • GDC guidance on advertising. Accurate, honest information, including whether you're NHS, mixed or private, and GDC registration details where required.
  • ASA & the CAP Code. No misleading claims, no unrealistic before-and-afters, and no promotion of prescription-only medicines such as Botox to the public.
  • UK data & email law. Patient emails and outreach handled in line with UK GDPR and PECR.
